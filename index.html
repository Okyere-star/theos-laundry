Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our laundry, pickup, and care standards.

Our standard turnaround time is 24 to 48 hours. Once we pick up your laundry, it goes straight to sorting, washing, drying, and folding before being delivered back to your door.

Yes! We offer an Express Same-Day Service for orders picked up before 10:00 AM. Your clothes will be processed with priority and delivered back to you by evening.

We separate darks, lights, and delicate fabrics before processing. You can also specify detergent preferences (e.g., hypoallergenic or scented) and starch levels during pickup.

We inventory every garment upon collection and track them throughout the entire cleaning process. In the rare event of damage or a missing item, our customer care team resolves it immediately per our garment replacement policy.

Simply schedule a time through our booking form or WhatsApp. Our driver arrives at your designated location, seals your garments in a laundry bag, and returns them clean and folded.
